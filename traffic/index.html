Cars
Each car is colored based on its speed, transitioning from red at 0 px/frame to green at 2 px/frame to cyan at >= 10 px/frame. The current average speed among all cars and current total length of all cars' planned paths are shown in the bottom right.

Click a car to select it. Additional information is shown when a car is selected:
  • The selected car is marked with a large black circle.
  • The car's planned path is highlighted in green.
  • Detected obstacles are marked with blue circles.
  • A cyan X marks the earliest point along the car's planned path that it could stop at, given its current speed and maximum acceleration.
  • The car's position on its current lane, current speed and max speed are shown in the bottom left. A simple graphical spedometer is shown in the bottom center.
