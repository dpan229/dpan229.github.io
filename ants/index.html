All trails are multiplied by this factor at the end of every frame. If this is low, trails will evaporate quickly; if this is high, trails will evaporate slowly.
Every frame, ants sample 5 points at this distance in front of themselves to determine how much to turn. A larger value will allow ants to see trails from further away, but make them less able to see trails that are close by.
Angular spread of the points sampled by each ant to determine how much to turn. A larger value will allow ants to turn more sharply.
Weight given to random angle when ants decide how much to turn on each frame. If this is low, ants will always follow trails; if this is high, ants will deviate more from trails.
